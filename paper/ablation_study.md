## H. Ablation Study

An ablation study was conducted to analyze the contribution of each component within the proposed Multi-Agent AI Cyber Defense Framework. Unlike conventional intrusion detection systems that focus solely on attack detection, the proposed architecture progressively introduces additional capabilities including threat intelligence generation, explainable decision support, trust-aware automation, and immutable forensic logging.

The Detection Agent provides the fundamental capability of distinguishing malicious activities from benign system behavior. The addition of the Threat Analysis Agent enables fine-grained attack categorization and threat intelligence generation. Incorporating the Explainability Agent improves transparency by identifying the key factors influencing attack classification decisions. The Trust Manager introduces confidence-based decision governance, allowing incidents to be automatically handled, reviewed by analysts, or escalated depending on the reliability of model predictions. Finally, the Blockchain Logger provides tamper-resistant storage of incident evidence and establishes a verifiable audit trail for cybersecurity investigations.

The ablation results demonstrate that each component contributes a distinct functionality that extends the framework beyond traditional intrusion detection approaches. While detection and classification provide core security intelligence, explainability, trust management, and blockchain logging improve transparency, accountability, and operational reliability. The complete framework therefore delivers a comprehensive cyber defense workflow that integrates detection, analysis, explanation, response, trust assessment, and forensic preservation within a unified architecture.
